      Welcome to the lab

      palm-lab watches your webcam for hand gestures and runs the shortcut you gave each one. Hold up a peace sign, and your music starts.

      • Hold, don’t wave. A gesture runs after you hold it still for a moment, so passing movements don’t count.
      • It stays on your PC. The camera picture is read on this computer and is never saved or sent anywhere.
      • You’re in charge. Pause tracking, switch any gesture off, or require both hands for extra safety.

      This experiment takes about two minutes: check the camera, measure your hand, test the gestures, and make your first shortcut.

      Calibrate the camera

      Raise one hand so palm-lab can find it. The dots and lines are how it sees your hand.

      Opening camera…

      • Sit about an arm’s length from the camera.
      • Have light on your hand, not behind it.
      • Wrong camera? Change it in Settings afterwards.

      Measure your hand

      Every hand bends a little differently. Hold each pose still while palm-lab measures, and it will tune itself to you.

      Open handfingers spread
      Fistfingers curled tight

      Test your gestures

      Make each gesture and hold it until its tube fills. Nothing runs yet; this only checks that palm-lab reads your hand.

      One won’t light up?
      • Turn your palm toward the camera.
      • Keep the fingers that should be up clearly apart.
      • For the thumbs up, point the thumb out to the side or up, away from your fingers.
      • Still stuck? Go back and measure your hand again.

      Your first experiment

      Pick a gesture, then drag a piece into it to say what it should do. Click a piece to add it, or drag it into place.
